## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a **Premium Dark Athletic** aesthetic built for elite sports representation, high-stakes combat management, and relentless athletic performance. The tone is aggressive yet prestigious, striking a balance between raw arena grit and boardroom authority. 

Key attributes:
- **Tenacious & Resilient:** Crisp edges, high-contrast dark environments, and immediate visual impact evoke the discipline of the cage, the ring, and the track.
- **Elite Representation:** Restrained use of metallic championship accents alongside rich carbon-toned surfaces signals top-tier financial and career stewardship for elite athletes.
- **Data-Driven Performance:** Numerical typography, stat telemetry, and compact athletic breakdowns take center stage with high-legibility tabular treatments.

The aesthetic fuses modern high-contrast digital interfaces with technical sportswear minimalism: pitch-black foundations, obsidian layered surfaces, hyper-vibrant crimson energetic accents, and disciplined championship gold highlights.

## Colors

The palette relies on a deep, light-absorbent base punctuated by high-kinetic accents.

- **Primary (`#FF2E4D` - Kinetic Crimson):** Represents raw output, knockout power, adrenaline, and critical active triggers. Applied to primary calls-to-action, record knockouts, live fight status indicators, and aggressive interactive feedback.
- **Secondary (`#F5A623` - Champion Gold):** Denotes world titles, championship records, premier division tiering, and milestone accolades. Used sparingly to preserve prestige.
- **Tertiary (`#16181D` - Carbon Surface):** Provides elevation against the background. Houses athletic rosters, telemetry panels, contracts, and athlete cards.
- **Neutral (`#0B0C10` - Arena Pitch):** The true-dark canvas base across the entire interface, minimizing light pollution and maximizing contrast.

### Supporting Monochromes & Semantic Hues
- **Text Primary (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast optical white for fight stats, display names, and critical text.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8` - Muted Slate):** Standard secondary text for weight classes, records breakdowns, and contract metadata.
- **Border / Outline (`#252932` - Graphite Rim):** Low-contrast structural lines to demarcate modules without clutter.
- **Victory Green (`#00E676`):** Dedicated to fight outcome badges (Wins, Defenses).
- **Defeat Gray (`#475569`):** Reserved for Losses/Draws in stat records.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates an unmistakable stadium presence.

- **Headlines & Metric Callouts (`Anton`):** Heavy, condensed, and assertive. Headings must always be rendered in full uppercase with slight tracking (`letter-spacing`) to maintain poster-like impact. Use Anton strictly for display banners, athlete surnames, stat values (e.g., "28-0-0"), and high-level card titles.
- **Body & Navigation (`Manrope`):** Delivers geometric precision, pristine legibility at small sizes, and modern neutral tone to balance the aggressive display font.
- **Labels & Microcopy (`Manrope`):** Rendered in uppercase with medium-to-bold weights and expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) for category tags, divisions (e.g., `LIGHTWEIGHT // 155 LBS`), and table column headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured **12-column fluid grid** for desktop and tablet, converting into a **4-column layout** on mobile devices.

### Form Factor Behavior
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 3rem screen margins. Used for multi-column athlete comparison grids, complex analytics, and wide contract negotiation matrices.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, 2rem margins. Roster cards stack 2-up; fighter profiles use a split-pane layout.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1.25rem margins. Single-column stacked layouts with horizontal swipe strips for telemetry counters and fight cards.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm follows a strict base-8 discipline. Element spacing inside cards is dense and compact (`space-xs` to `space-md`), mimicking broadcast sports graphics, while section boundaries enjoy generous breathing room (`space-xl` and above) to keep the dark aesthetic crisp and uncrowded.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid murky shadows in a pure dark theme, depth is achieved through **tonal layering**, **specular edge highlights**, and **concentrated energy glows**.

1. **Base Layer (Ground - Level 0):** Hex `#0B0C10` (Arena Pitch). Used for canvas background and full-bleed containers.
2. **Surface Layer (Cards / Panels - Level 1):** Hex `#16181D` (Carbon Surface). Paired with a subtle 1px border of `#252932`. No dropped shadows.
3. **Elevated / Interactive Layer (Hover / Modals - Level 2):** Hex `#1F222A` (Obsidian Light). Uses a subtle colored rim:
   - Primary Interactive Rim: 1px solid `rgba(255, 46, 77, 0.4)`
   - Gold Championship Rim: 1px solid `rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.4)`
4. **Energy Overlays & Glows:** Soft, directional drop shadows are applied solely to active states and primary buttons:
   - `box-shadow: 0px 8px 24px rgba(255, 46, 77, 0.28)`
5. **Glass Frosting:** Used strictly for sticky navigation bars and modal sheets: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with `background: rgba(11, 12, 16, 0.85)` and an understated bottom divider.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft-Chiseled Shape System (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and sub-cards utilize a minimal **0.25rem (4px)** radius.
- Larger roster cards, event displays, and analytics panels scale up to **0.5rem (8px)** (`rounded-lg`).
- Modals, action drawers, and hero banners feature a maximum corner radius of **0.75rem (12px)** (`rounded-xl`).

Circular radii are explicitly prohibited for containers, cards, and buttons to preserve the aggressive, structured sports identity. Full rounding (`rounded-full`) is reserved solely for micro-status dots (e.g., active sparring indicator, live match beacon) and circular athlete portrait thumbs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Button:** High-visibility `#FF2E4D` fill, pure white uppercase `Anton` or `Manrope 700` typography, 4px border radius. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. On hover, elevates with a 4px upward translation and red ambient blur.
- **Secondary (Contract/Outline) Button:** Transparent background, 1.5px border in `#252932`, white text. Transitions on hover to `#1F222A` fill and `#FFFFFF` border.
- **Championship Tier Button:** Gold outline `#F5A623`, text `#F5A623`. Used exclusively for title fight promotions, VIP experiences, and signature contracts.

### Sport Cards & Athlete Profiles
- Built on `#16181D` surface with a 1px `#252932` perimeter.
- Top section: full-bleed duotone athlete imagery with bottom gradient fading into card surface.
- Content zone: Condensed uppercase athlete surname in `Anton` (`headline-lg`), weight division in muted slate (`label-sm`), and a three-column stat pill bar (e.g., `RECORD`, `KO%`, `REACH`).

### Stat Counters & Telemetry Modules
- Numbers rendered in `Anton` (`headline-xl` or `display-lg`), accompanied by a micro label in `label-sm` tracking.
- Positive trends/finishes highlighted in `#00E676` or `#FF2E4D`; milestone records highlighted in `#F5A623`.
- Subtle vertical divider lines in `#252932` between adjacent stat metrics.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact, 2px radius, uppercase `label-sm`.
  - *Title Holder / Champion:* Background `rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.15)`, text `#F5A623`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.3)`.
  - *Main Event / Active Camp:* Background `rgba(255, 46, 77, 0.15)`, text `#FF2E4D`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 46, 77, 0.3)`.
  - *Division Tag:* Background `#1F222A`, text `#94A3B8`, no border.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Solid dark base (`#0B0C10`), 1px outline (`#252932`), 4px border radius, white text. Focus state transforms the border to `#FF2E4D` with no outer glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Square form-factors with 2px radius. Inactive border is `#475569`. Active state fills with `#FF2E4D` and a crisp white tick mark.
- **Toggle Switches:** Angular 4px rounded track (`#252932`) with an internal rectangular thumb shifting to `#FF2E4D` when active.

### Athlete Tale-of-the-Tape Comparator
- A dual-column vs. component split down the center by a slim vertical crimson divider. Symmetrical stat comparisons (height, reach, stance, win methods) with winning/dominant numbers surfaced in pure white and lesser metrics shifted to muted slate (`#94A3B8`).
